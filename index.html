School360 Development Progress

A mobile-first education management platform designed for institutions from Kindergarten through postgraduate and professional education.

Repository checkpoint: 25 November 2026 · Active development branch: integration/institution-management-app
Development status: Early implementation. The private repository contains the Laravel 13 and Filament 5 application foundation, institution and platform identity work, tenant-separated data modules, and an expanding set of academic, operational, and portal services. Recent portal and communication additions are committed but still awaiting the consolidated CI verification phase. This is not a production service.

  • Scheduled announcement publication, tenant-resolved audience distribution and in-app delivery processing
